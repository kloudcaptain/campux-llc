Accessibility statement

Last updated: August 2026

Campux LLC wants campux.co to be usable by everyone, including people who rely on assistive technology. This statement describes what we have done, what we know is not yet right, and how to reach us.

Standard

We aim to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at level AA.

What we have done

The site uses semantic HTML with landmarks, one heading per page at the top level, and a logical heading order. Every interactive element has a visible focus state. Text and background colors meet a 4.5:1 contrast ratio. Motion is limited to one diagram animation and is disabled when your system asks for reduced motion. Forms have labels, and errors are shown next to the field. Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative drawings are marked so screen readers skip them. The site works without JavaScript except for the article filter and the form's pre-selection, which fall back to a full list and an empty form.

Known limitations

We have not yet completed a third-party accessibility audit, so this list may not be complete.

Testing

This site has not yet undergone formal third-party accessibility testing.

Feedback

If something on this site does not work for you, email hello@campux.co and tell us the page and what happened. We will reply within five business days and fix what we can.
