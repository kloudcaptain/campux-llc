Practices

What we do

Our core work is cloud landing zones and governance for public-sector and regulated organizations. The other four practices make that platform secure, automated, cost-controlled and easy for your team to run.

Architecture & landing zones

Management groups, subscriptions, identity, network

We design Azure landing zones aligned to the Cloud Adoption Framework, with hub-and-spoke or Virtual WAN networking, and we plan migrations with rehearsed cutovers. On AWS we build Control Tower and multi-account landing zones. In both cases the environment is written in Bicep or Terraform and committed to your repository.

  • Azure landing zone design
  • AWS Control Tower & multi-account setup
  • Hybrid connectivity
  • Tenant, subscription & account strategy
  • Migration & cutover planning

DevSecOps

Pipelines, policy-as-code, Defender, Zero Trust

We build Azure DevOps or GitHub Actions pipelines with code scanning, secret scanning and policy gates, so every change is checked before it ships. Defender for Cloud and Zero Trust baselines are configured as part of the platform. On AWS we use Security Hub, AWS Config and IAM Access Analyzer for the same controls.

  • CI/CD design & release gates
  • Azure Policy & initiatives
  • Defender for Cloud & Defender for APIs
  • Supply chain: SBOM, dependency & secret scanning
  • Key Vault & workload identity
  • API Management & Front Door

Automation & AI-assisted operations

Runbooks, workflows, integrations, document agents

We automate the recurring work that keeps a platform running: environment refreshes, access reviews, drift checks, reporting, and ticket or document handling. For platform tasks we use Azure Automation and PowerShell. For business workflows we use Logic Apps, Azure Functions and Power Automate, or Lambda, EventBridge and Step Functions on AWS. Where it is useful, we add AI-assisted agents for document intake and summarization, always with a person reviewing the output.

  • Azure Automation & PowerShell runbooks
  • Logic Apps · Functions · Power Automate
  • AI document & intake agents
  • Monitoring, alerting & auto-remediation

FinOps & cost governance

Spend analysis, right-sizing, guardrails

We analyze spend, right-size resources and review reservations, then put budgets, alerts and tagging policy in place so the savings hold. Your finance team gets a monthly report they can read without an engineer. Azure and AWS.

  • Spend & usage analysis
  • Right-sizing & reservations
  • Budget & alert governance
  • Tagging & showback

Training & curriculum

Instructor-led, live lab tenants

Every build ends with your staff trained to run what we built, on lab environments that mirror your tenant. Where a client wants more, we deliver a short instructor-led program written around their tools.

  • Custom instructor-led programs
  • Lab tenant design
  • Certification tracks (AZ-104, AZ-400)
  • Runbook authoring with your staff

Method

How an engagement runs

Every engagement starts with a paid, fixed-scope architecture review. If the findings do not justify a build, you still keep the report. If they do, we build, hand over, and stay on as advisors.

Durations shown are typical for a mid-size tenant; county-scale migrations with change control and rehearsed cutovers run longer, and we say so in the proposal.

  1. Weeks 1–2

    Review

    Tenant, identity, network, pipelines and cost. Written findings a non-engineer can read.

  2. Weeks 3–10

    Build

    Landing zone, pipelines and guardrails as reviewed, versioned code in your repositories.

  3. Weeks 8–12

    Govern

    Policy, Defender and budget alerts enforcing the design continuously.

  4. Final weeks

    Hand over

    Runbooks your staff wrote, lab time on a mirror of your tenant, a certification path if wanted.
