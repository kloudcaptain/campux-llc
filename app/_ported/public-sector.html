How to engage

Three ways agencies and primes work with us

Direct award

For work under your simplified-acquisition or small-purchase threshold, we can be engaged directly on a purchase order against a written scope. Reviews and training programs usually fit here.

You will need from us: capability statement, W-9, certificate of insurance, and a fixed-price quote.

Subcontract to a prime

On larger task orders we work as a named subcontractor: senior Azure or AWS hands, documentation discipline, and small-business status for the prime's plan.

You will need from us: capability statement, staffing roles and rates, past-performance narrative, and signed flow-down terms.

Teaming

For opportunities that need more than one firm, we team with primes and other small businesses and take a defined workstream, usually the platform, pipeline or governance piece.

You will need from us: teaming agreement, workshare description, and our identifiers for the proposal.

What we deliver for government IT

The same work, with the constraints agencies actually have

Landing zones with policy inheritance

Management groups and Azure Policy so every new subscription is compliant when it is created, with the evidence an auditor can pull without a meeting.

Azure Government and AWS GovCloud

We design for the commercial or government cloud your data classification requires and document what changes between them. We work in Azure commercial today and do not yet have Azure Government or AWS GovCloud production experience. Where a contract requires it, we say so and team with a firm that holds the authorization.

Handover your staff can run

Runbooks written by your team during the build, training on a mirror of your tenant, and a retained advisory line afterward. County IT keeps the platform; the vendor does not.

Compliance and controls

Built to the control baselines your auditor already uses

We are not an authorizing body and we do not hold an ATO. We build your Azure environment to recognized control baselines, enforce them with Azure Policy, and hand you the evidence, so your compliance team starts from a documented position rather than a blank page.

NIST SP 800-53

Landing zones mapped to 800-53 rev 5 control families and enforced with the built-in Azure Policy regulatory-compliance initiatives, exported as compliance evidence.

NIST SP 800-171

For CUI environments, controls aligned to 800-171, with the policy state and configuration evidence your SSP and POA&M need.

CJIS Security Policy

For Georgia state, local and law-enforcement work, we design to the CJIS Security Policy: advanced authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, audit logging, and personnel-screening controls.
