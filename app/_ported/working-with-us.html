Engagement models

Fixed price, fixed scope, two weeks. Tenant, identity, network, pipelines and cost. Written findings a non-engineer can read. You keep the report whether or not you go further.

Fixed price or T&M against a signed scope. Milestones are tied to working code in your repositories, not to documents. Rehearsed cutovers for anything that moves production.

Retained advisory

Monthly, after handover. A block of hours, quarterly governance and cost review, and priority response for changes and incidents. Tiers below.

Continuity, commercial, compliance

What a buyer usually checks

Continuity and delivery

Two principals; every engagement is led by one and known to both. All code, pipelines and documentation live in your repositories from week one. Surge and specialist capacity arranged through teaming per opportunity, disclosed in the proposal. US-based personnel; background checks and client onboarding on request.

Commercial

Georgia LLC. SAM.gov active. W-9 and NDA on request. Net-30 standard; purchase-order and subcontract terms accepted. Fixed price where the scope is stable, T&M where it is not. Client-approved travel at cost.

Compliance

Least-privilege access, MFA, no standing credentials, access removed at close. Professional and cyber liability coverage is being put in place; a certificate will be available before contract award. Azure commercial today; not yet operating in Azure Government.

What we don't do

Where we will refer you elsewhere

Google Cloud delivery. Greenfield AWS platforms for organizations with no existing AWS footprint. Desktop support, help desk or break-fix. Software license resale. Staff augmentation without an outcome attached. If that is what you need, we will say so on the first call and refer you where we can.

Questions

Frequently asked

How fast can you start?
A review can usually start within two weeks of a signed scope. Builds depend on access and change control; we give a start date in the proposal, not a range.
Fixed price or time and materials?
Reviews and training are fixed price. Builds are fixed price when the scope is stable and T&M against a signed scope when it is not. We say which and why in the proposal.
Who owns the code?
You do. Infrastructure code, pipelines, policy, runbooks and documentation are committed to your repositories as they are produced. We keep our methods and templates and license anything embedded in your deliverables to you.
What if a principal is unavailable?
Every engagement is led by one principal and known to both. Documentation is written as we go, not at the end.
Will you sign our MSA or purchase-order terms?
Yes, subject to review. Government terms and governing law control where they must; our terms fill in only what the agreement leaves out.
Do you carry insurance?
We are putting professional and cyber liability coverage in place. A certificate of insurance will be available before contract award.
Do you support Azure Government or AWS GovCloud?
Not yet. We work in Azure commercial today. Where a contract requires Azure Government, we say so and team with a firm that holds the authorization.
Do you do AWS?
Yes, for clients who already run on AWS. We do not take on greenfield AWS platforms for organizations with no AWS footprint, and we do not work in Google Cloud.
