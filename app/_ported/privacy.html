Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Note for the owner: this draft is written to match what the new campux.co actually does. It is not legal advice. Have it checked before publication, and update the bracketed items.

Who we are

Campux LLC is a cloud consulting and IT training firm organized in the State of Georgia, United States. This policy explains what personal information we collect through campux.co, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Questions go to hello@campux.co.

What we collect

When you contact us. If you use the contact form, book a call, email us, or phone us, we collect what you give us: your name, organization, work email, phone number if you provide it, and whatever you tell us about your environment or requirements.

When you visit the site. Our hosting provider records standard server logs, which include your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers on this site.

When we work for you. During an engagement we may handle data inside your Azure or AWS environment. That data belongs to you. It is covered by our agreement with you, not by this policy, and we use it only to deliver the agreed work.

We do not buy personal data, and we do not collect more than we need to respond to you.

Why we collect it

We use contact information to reply to your inquiry, to scope work you have asked about, and to deliver services you have engaged us for. We use server logs to keep the site running and secure. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not add you to a mailing list. If we ever start one, joining it will be a separate, explicit choice.

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone for marketing.

We use a small number of service providers to run this site and our business. Each one processes data only to provide its service to us:

  • Form handling: a Cloudflare Worker that emails your message to us, delivered via Resend.
  • Call booking: no scheduling tool is used on this site.
  • Web hosting: Cloudflare.
  • Email: contact-form messages are delivered by Resend.
  • Fonts: loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights or safety of Campux, our clients, or others.

Cookies

The site uses only the cookies it needs to function. There are no advertising cookies and no third-party tracking cookies. Because we do not set non-essential cookies, we do not show a cookie banner.

How long we keep it

We keep contact inquiries for up to two years from your last message, or until you ask us to delete them. Server logs are kept for 30 days. Records connected to a signed engagement are kept for the life of the engagement and afterward for as long as tax, contract, or regulatory obligations require.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email hello@campux.co and we will respond within thirty days. If you are a resident of a state whose privacy law gives you additional rights, we will honor those rights as the law requires. We do not treat anyone differently for exercising them.

Security

We protect the information we hold with the same controls we recommend to clients: least-privilege access, multi-factor authentication, encrypted storage and transport, and logging. No system is perfectly secure, and we will tell you promptly if we learn that your information has been compromised.

Visitors outside the United States

This site is operated from the United States and your information is stored and processed here. If you contact us from another country, you are sending your information to the United States.

Children

This site is for businesses and government organizations. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date at the top and post a notice on the site.
